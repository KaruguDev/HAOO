> updated during execution.
>
> **On the ordering of the two D-04 gates.** Gate 1 (the lockdown readback) and gate 2 (the
> wire-level payload regression) are deliberately in different waves. Gate 2 asserts the
> payload that `before_send` actually returns while the full HAOO journey is driven through
> a mounted page — it cannot be written before the adapter it observes exists, and the
> adapter ships in `04.1-04` (wave 3). So gate 2 lands in `04.1-05` Task 2 at wave 4, one
> wave after the tracer. This is not a sampling gap: the tracer in `04.1-04` carries its own
> end-to-end payload assertion over one path, and `04.1-05` widens it to every path. The row
> below marked `W0*` is that intentional case.
>
> **On `04.1-04` task numbering.** `04.1-04` is a single-task plan: its rollout-ordering
> question was answered at planning time as `remove-now` per D-01 and ROADMAP success
> criterion 1, so the checkpoint that once stood ahead of the tracer is gone and the tracer
> is Task 1.
>
> **On `-t` filters and pre-existing describes.** `vitest -t` exits 0 when its pattern
> matches nothing, and exits 0 just as happily when it matches only describes this phase
> never touches. Every command below therefore names a describe that the attributed task
> creates or modifies, and is a command that task's own acceptance criteria run. Three
> filters were corrected at this revision for failing that rule: `-t "campaign whole-value
> allowlist"` and `-t "exact stored schema"` (both unchanged Phase 3 describes that no task
> in `04.1-05` edits) and `-t "provenance"` (which matches only cases `04.1-07` Task 1
> rewrites, none of which assert the report text the row named). A fourth, `-t "disclosure"`,
> was narrowed: it matched two unchanged describes as well as the new group.
>
> **Pre-existing coverage retained but not mapped.** `campaign whole-value allowlist` and
> `exact stored schema` in `src/test/measurement.test.ts` survive Phase 3 unchanged and no
> task in this phase edits them. They are real coverage — `04.1-05` records both as existing
> coverage in its own flagged-assumptions table — but they belong to the phase-level
> `npm test` gate, not to a per-task row, because no task of this phase can turn them red or
> green.
