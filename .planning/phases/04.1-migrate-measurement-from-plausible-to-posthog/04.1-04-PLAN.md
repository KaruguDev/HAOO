<objective>
Make "confirmed automatic-capture opt-out" mean what D-04 says it means: two independent
gates, not one.

Gate 1 is a configuration assertion at the initialization boundary that fails closed at
runtime — that is what keeps 04-09's guarantee failing closed in production. Gate 2 is a
network-payload regression over every event path — that is what catches a version bump or
option rename that gate 1 would pass. Phase 4 needed three gap-closure rounds on exactly
this class of drift, so one gate is demonstrably not enough.

This plan also restores the six provider describes to full depth against PostHog (the
tracer left them at tracer depth), makes the D-05 refusal owner-visible, and re-arms the
Phase 4 gap-1 page-unmount regression — which lives on the enablement path this phase turns
on for the first time.

Purpose: prove the lockdown twice, independently, and prove a refusal never costs the
visitor the journey.
Output: a full-depth provider test surface, a wire-level regression, and a visible refusal.
</objective>
