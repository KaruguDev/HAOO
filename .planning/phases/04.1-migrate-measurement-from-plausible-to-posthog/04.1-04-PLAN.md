## Recorded planning decision — rollout ordering is `remove-now`

**This is settled at planning time. There is no choice for the executor to make here and no
checkpoint gating this plan.** Plausible is removed in the same commit that proves PostHog:
the adapter, its approved-origin entry, its independently-pinned preload fixture, its build
wiring, and its provider-union member all go in the commit that lands the tracer.

Why it is already decided, and why re-asking would be wrong:

- **D-01 locks the outcome.** "Full replacement... after this phase no Plausible adapter,
  approved-source entry, preload-contract fixture, test, environment variable, or owner
  instruction remains, and `MEASUREMENT_PROVIDERS` is `'none' | 'posthog'`." Any variant
  that carries Plausible past this phase leaves a three-member provider union standing at
  phase exit, which D-01 forbids outright.
- **ROADMAP success criterion 1 restates the same thing** as a phase-exit condition, and
  `04.1-08` Task 2 enforces it with a repository-wide negative grep.
- **04.1-CONTEXT's `<deferred>` block assigned this question to planning, not to the
  owner** — rollout ordering is "left to research and planning within the boundary above
  rather than deferred to a later phase". Handing it back would re-open a decision the
  owner already closed.
- **Nothing operational is at stake.** Plausible was never activated in production: no live
  project, no dashboard, no history, no data. The fallback that a delayed removal would
  preserve has never delivered a single event either.

A note for anyone reading a structure-validation warning on this plan: `verify.plan-structure`
reports that the `one-way` task below has no preceding `checkpoint:decision`. That is expected
and correct here. `gsd-core/references/planner-reversibility.md` names it directly under
Anti-patterns — *"Gating a decision already made: if the phase CONTEXT.md records the human
choosing this exact option, the door is already walked through. Keep the rating for the
record; do not insert a checkpoint to re-ask."* 04.1-CONTEXT D-01 is that record. The rating
stays; the gate does not.

The cost of this ordering, stated plainly so the executor is not surprised by it: between
this plan and the live one-action-one-event gate re-opened in `04.1-08`, the only evidence
PostHog works is fixture-driven, and recovery from a failed live gate is forward (fix
PostHog) rather than backward (re-enable Plausible). That cost is accepted, and it is what
the `<reversibility>` element on the task below records for the phase history.
