<objective>
The tracer slice: one HAOO event travels from a visitor action, through the unchanged
measurement facade, through a newly-installed PostHog adapter whose automatic-capture
lockdown is proven by a merged-configuration readback, and leaves the page as a bare name
carrying exactly three transport properties — with the Plausible adapter removed in the
same commit.

This is the phase's architectural risk in one path. PostHog's defaults are the opposite of
every privacy decision this project has made, part of its feature set is controlled by a
server-side remote-configuration fetch, and it attaches roughly thirty default properties
to every capture. Proving one path end-to-end here is what makes the remaining expansion
mechanical.

Purpose: prove the seam holds against a large multi-product SDK before building out on it.
Output: `posthog-lockdown.ts`, `posthog.ts`, the migrated product/type/build configuration,
the facade swap, and every Plausible artifact removed.
</objective>
