# Phase 04.1 Plan 01: Restate the Boundary Suite for a Bundled SDK Summary

**The bundle-level `posthog` prohibition is withdrawn with `PROVIDER_INGESTION_HOST_SOURCE_FORBIDDEN` named as its successor at the production-source site, identity/queue prohibitions are relocated off the vendor bundle, and an adapter-independent fixture pins PostHog's `{uuid, event, properties}` capture payload against the vendor's own documented defaults.**

## Accomplishments

- The boundary suite now admits a bundled analytics SDK without a single remaining claim
  becoming false. `UNCONDITIONAL_ANALYTICS_ORIGINS_FORBIDDEN` is narrowed to competitor
  origins (`googletagmanager`, `google-analytics`, `umami`, `segment.com`) and still applied
  at both sites.
- The withdrawal of `04-08`'s delivery-mechanism guarantee is recorded in-source rather than
  performed by deletion. `PROVIDER_INGESTION_HOST_SOURCE_FORBIDDEN` (`/us\.i\.posthog\.com/i`)
  is applied only over production source, and its doc comment states what it proves (the
  ingestion host literal never enters a module under `src/`; it is repository-owned data
  reaching a bundle only through the provider-gated build-time constant), what it no longer
  proves (the built bundle necessarily carries the vendor's own default host, because the SDK
  is imported rather than fetched from an approved script origin), and that this is an
  explicit withdrawal of `04-08` with the successor named.
- `MEASUREMENT_IDENTITY_SOURCE_FORBIDDEN` moves the two identity/queue patterns from the
  built-bundle scan to the production-source scan, with the relocation rationale recorded:
  asserting them against a minified vendor artifact would be a claim about the vendor, while
  asserting them against `src/` is exactly the claim MEAS-02 and MEAS-03 depend on.
- `REPORT_CREDENTIAL_BUNDLE_FORBIDDEN` re-pointed to `/POSTHOG_QUERY_API_KEY/` and
  `/\/api\/projects\/[^/]*\/query/`; `/Authorization/` and `/Bearer\s/` kept unchanged,
  because research verified both are absent from `posthog-js@1.425.1/dist/module.js`.
- `src/test/fixtures/posthog-capture-contract.ts` transcribes `VendorCaptureResult`
  (`{uuid, event, properties}`), the merged-config readback surface, and a stub client whose
  `init` starts from the **vendor's documented defaults** — `autocapture: true`,
  `person_profiles: 'identified_only'`, `persistence: 'localStorage+cookie'`,
  `advanced_disable_flags: false` and the rest — so a readback assertion written against it in
  `04.1-04` cannot be vacuous. It returns the instance without throwing on a blank token,
  which is precisely why the readback is the gate rather than the absence of a throw.

## Decisions Made

- **Rename the provider-unset bundle case.** Its old title claimed "identity, property, queue,
  SDK, or credential seams"; after the relocation it asserts competitor origins, credential
  shapes and the payload-shape pattern only. Leaving the title would have been the same failure
  mode the plan exists to prevent — a claim stated more strongly than what is actually checked.
  Grep confirmed the title has no external consumers (unlike
  `keeps analytics origins out of production source modules`, which `04-05-SUMMARY.md` cites as
  a coverage ref and which was therefore left byte-identical).
- **Keep the source-scan case's title and widen its contents.** The identity group is scanned
  inside `keeps analytics origins out of production source modules` rather than in a new case,
  preserving that coverage ref; a comment at the call site explains why the bundle is the wrong
  subject.
- **Declare `InstalledVendorPostHogClient` as an extension** rather than bolting inspection
  helpers onto `VendorPostHogClient`. The vendor's own shape stays exactly what the vendor
  documents; everything the fixture adds for tests is visibly the fixture's.
- **Assemble a realistically-enriched payload in the stub's `capture`** (transport keys plus
  `$current_url`, `$referrer`, `$lib`). A stub whose payload arrived already reduced to
  bare-name form would prove nothing about a chokepoint.
