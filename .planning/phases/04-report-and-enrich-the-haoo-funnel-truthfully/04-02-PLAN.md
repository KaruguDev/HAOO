<objective>
Attach a disclosed, human-readable engagement summary to voluntarily submitted HAOO qualification
enquiries, built only from the Phase 3 bounded browser context and normalized campaign snapshot.

**As a** HAOO product owner, **I want to** see a short readable paragraph of coarse engagement
context beside each enquiry, **so that** I can reply usefully without a hidden score and without
anyone being tracked as an identified person.

Purpose: MEAS-05 requires the summary; Phase 3 verification deferred exactly this attachment to
Phase 4. MEAS-08 requires that what the summary claims stays inside what a browser can observe.
Output: a pure product-generic formatter, HAOO's byte-exact sentence configuration, the reserved
email label, and the wiring that puts the field on the existing FormSubmit request.
</objective>
