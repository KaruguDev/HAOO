<objective>
Run the accessibility rule engine against the live surfaces for the first time in this project's
history, record everything it returns, and classify it — without failing the run.

This is deliberately exploratory and non-gating, and deliberately early. No such scan has ever been
performed against either site, the shipped palette has never been measured for text contrast, and the
contrast rule is one of the impacts the owner decided blocks the gate. A phase that discovers a
blocking finding at its finish line has no room to act on it; this plan finds them while plan 05-14
still has a wave to fix them in.

Purpose: turn "we do not know what the live sites score" into a recorded number with provenance.
Output: `evidence/axe-baseline.json`, and a classified triage list in `05-EVIDENCE-AXE.md`.

**Working directory: the HAOO checkout** (05-01, D-01/D-04).
</objective>

    cannot be reached live: in-flight, success, transport failure and blocked, each induced by routing
