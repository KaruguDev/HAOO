## Planner assumptions carried forward (unresolved probe edges — surfaced, not dropped)

- **LEAD-07 / concurrency — "If interrupted or run in parallel, what is guaranteed?" (unresolved).**
  The planner's assumption: the mail chain is a strictly serial, single-operator sequence with no
  concurrency guarantee of any kind. Nothing prevents a second operator from adding, changing or
  removing an MX record, or from sending a second submission, while this chain is in flight. The
  mitigation is procedural rather than mechanical: every link records its own measurement timestamp,
  so an out-of-order or interleaved run is *visible* in the record even though it is not prevented.
  If this chain is interrupted between links, it is restarted from link 1 (a fresh `dig`), never
  resumed from a recorded value. Carried to plan 05-16, which owns links 2 and 3.
