## Planner assumptions carried forward (unresolved probe edges — surfaced, not dropped)

- **LEAD-07 / concurrency (unresolved).** Restated from plan 05-02 because this plan is where it
  bites hardest: nothing mechanically prevents a second submission arriving in the mailbox from
  another source while this chain is in flight, and nothing serialises the two live submissions this
  phase sends. The guarantee is procedural, not enforced: exactly two live submissions are sent in
  the whole phase, each carries a distinct marker prefix, and each is recorded with its own
  timestamp — so an interleaving is *visible* in the record rather than prevented. If this plan and
  plan 05-16 were ever run concurrently, the two markers still distinguish the messages, but the
  ordering claim D-11 makes would be void and both links would have to be re-established.

- **Assumption A6 (from research).** FormSubmit's activation, once clicked, is assumed to make the
  endpoint permanently active with no expiry and no per-origin binding. If that assumption is wrong,
  the tagged submission in plan 05-16 could still fail after a successful activation — and D-11's
  two-record structure is precisely what turns that from a mystery into the diagnosis "activated,
  then not delivered".
