<objective>
Close the phase: run every enumerated check in both repositories against a deployment that includes
this phase's fixes, close the two standing blockers that work can close, put the two that only the
owner can close to the owner, and write the single record that says what this phase actually proved.

Two disciplines carry the whole plan. **Measured, not remembered** — every exit code in the final
table is produced by a run inside this plan, because a gate recorded green three waves ago is a
memory, not evidence. And **status follows evidence** — a requirement is complete because something
was measured, never because its plans executed.

Purpose: the phase's product is the credibility of its green marks. This is where that credibility is
either established or honestly qualified.
Output: `05-EVIDENCE.md`, the reconciled Phase 4 contract, and truthful requirement statuses.

**Working directory: the HAOO checkout** (05-01, D-01/D-04). `../ZERO-PAPERHUB` is the sibling.
