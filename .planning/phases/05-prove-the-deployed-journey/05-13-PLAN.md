<objective>
Prove QUAL-03's zoom and motion half: at 200% the HAOO page reflows without losing content or
controls, and with reduced motion requested the one shipped transition is suppressed without anything
disappearing with it.

Two precision points govern this plan. The halved-viewport method is correct and is the owner's
recorded matrix, but it models **200%**, which is a resize-text-class claim — the reflow criterion is
defined at a 320-pixel width, which is why the closed viewport list carries that entry separately.
Labelling each measurement with the criterion it actually measures is not pedantry; an evidence file
claiming reflow conformance from a 640-pixel measurement would be wrong. And the reduced-motion
assertions are as much about what must **not** change as what must: suppression that removed content
or controls would be a regression dressed as an accessibility feature.

Purpose: a visitor who zooms or who has asked for less motion gets the same journey, not a smaller
or emptier one.
Output: `e2e/zoom-motion.e2e.ts` and `05-EVIDENCE-ZOOM-MOTION.md`.

**Working directory: the HAOO checkout** (05-01, D-01/D-04).
</objective>

## Backstop items carried into `must_haves` (not silently dropped)

Two rows from the UI design contract are held out as **backstop** items rather than asserted, and
both appear in `must_haves.truths` with that marker so verification routes them to human judgement
rather than passing them silently:

- **Paragraph copy at a halved desktop viewport.** The per-box truncation check covers headings and
  primary actions, but whether paragraph text inside the shipped maximum-width content columns still
  reads well at a 640-pixel effective viewport is a line-length judgement no assertion settles.
- **The brochure HTML equivalent at the narrowest width and at 200%.** This is the densest text block
  on the page and the block QUAL-03's brochure-equivalent claim depends on. It is mechanically covered
  for escape and truncation; whether it stays *readable and complete* once ten capability cards
  collapse to one column and reflow again is a judgement that would be faked by any assertion that
  passed on unreadable output.

Task 3 captures the measured inputs for both — column widths, effective line lengths, card counts per
row — so the human judgement is informed rather than blind.
