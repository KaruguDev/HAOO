## Decisions Made

- **The asset move belongs to this plan.** `04.2-02-PLAN.md` step C says to leave the four asset paths, the PDF alternate link, the five recovery links and both SHA-256 pins "exactly as they are" — but that text was written against the planner's *recommended default*, and the owner chose otherwise. Under decision (b), recorded in `04.2-SPLIT-CONTRACT.md § Published document path`, all of them are edits. They had to land in the same commit as the moved files, because the tests assert file existence at the pinned paths.
- **`brochure/`, never `assets/`.** Carried the contract's reasoning into the suite as a comment on the new `ASSET_DIR` constant, because it governs any future asset move in either repository: `assets/` is Vite's default `build.assetsDir`, `BUILD_OUTPUTS` reads *every* file under `dist/assets` unfiltered, and the credential scan then reads each one as text.
- **The absorbed icon assertion derives its path** from the document's own `rel="icon"` `href` rather than restating `/brochure/haoo-logo.png`. A restated literal would keep passing after the icon was re-pointed elsewhere.
- **Both SHA-256 pins are byte-unchanged hex.** `git diff HEAD~1 HEAD -- src/test/build-output.test.ts | grep -c '^[-+].*SHA256'` is `0`. A move does not change content; what moved is the three path constants those digests are compared against, and the suite re-verified both digests against the files at their new paths.
- **One checkout, for the whole phase, at `$HAOO_CHECKOUT`.** A future plan needing concurrent access takes a `git worktree`, never a second clone — two plans building into the same `dist/` would make every artifact assertion in this phase's primary invariant carrier nondeterministic.
- **HTTPS enforcement was not touched.** It was already `true` on the pre-existing Pages site and the certificate already covers both legs; 04.2-09 owns that gate.
