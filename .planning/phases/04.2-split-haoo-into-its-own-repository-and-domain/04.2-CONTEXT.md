# Phase 04.2: Split HAOO into its Own Repository and Domain - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-09-05
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

HAOO moves out of the ZERO-PAPERHUB repository into its own repository, published at its own
domain `haoo.online`, with every privacy and boundary guarantee established by Phases 3 and 04.1
arriving intact and provably enforced in the new repository.

This phase moves and re-points. It does not change what HAOO does, add product capability, or
re-open a settled measurement or disclosure decision — except where a decision is made false by
the change of origin, in which case it is restated rather than silently carried.

**Explicitly out of scope:** proving the deployed journey (Phase 5), fixing the four open Phase 2
review blockers, and any change to the ZERO-PAPER HUB home page beyond removing HAOO source and
feeding its Products card.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Repository Provenance and History

- **D-01:** The new HAOO repository is created by cloning ZERO-PAPERHUB and pruning the non-HAOO
  half, keeping all 412 commits. `git blame` and `git log --follow` continue to work on every HAOO
  file, so the rationale encoded across Phase 1–04.1 — particularly the 04.1 gap-closure rounds that
  narrowed bundle invariants to named successors — stays reachable from the code it constrains.
  Accepted cost: HAOO's history contains commits that no longer touch anything in its tree, and
  roughly 32MB of `.git`.
  — **Reversibility:** one-way — once the pruned clone is pushed and worked on, re-deriving a
  different history shape means rewriting every SHA in the new repository, and 04.1 documents cite
  commit SHAs directly.

- **D-02:** ZERO-PAPERHUB remains the existing repository — it keeps its identity, issues, and
  history. HAOO is a newly created repository. HAOO source is removed from ZERO-PAPERHUB by an
  ordinary deleting commit, NOT by history rewriting. Past commits keep the HAOO files; the working
  tree does not. SPLT-01 is a claim about what each repository builds and ships, and that claim
  stays true under a normal deletion.
  — **Reversibility:** reversible — a deleting commit can be reverted; the alternative (filter-repo)
  would have force-pushed over every existing clone and broken the SHA citations in `.planning/`.

- **D-03:** `.planning/` moves whole to the HAOO repository. All five phases are HAOO work, so the
  GSD record follows the product. ZERO-PAPERHUB starts with a clean `.planning/` when it next needs
  one.
  — **Reversibility:** reversible — the directory is documentation; copying it back costs nothing
  but divergence.

### Shell Ownership and the Reuse Guarantee

- **D-04:** The product shell — `src/pages/ProductPage.tsx`, `ProductHeader`, `BrochurePanel`,
  `OnboardingChoices`, `QualifyForm`, `QualifyFallback`, `MeasurementDisclosure`,
  `qualify-form.logic.ts`, `src/products/copy.ts`, `src/products/types.ts`,
  `src/products/engagement-summary.ts` — moves wholesale into the HAOO repository. No shared package
  is published. The shell's product-generic discipline is retained, not dissolved: HAOO literals stay
  confined to `src/products/haoo.ts` (Phase 3 D-04), and that confinement remains enforced by test.
  — **Reversibility:** costly — extracting a package later means re-establishing a publish pipeline
  and a version contract, but the discipline preserved here is exactly what keeps that extraction
  possible.

- **D-05:** PROD-06's reuse claim is RETIRED WITH A NAMED SUCCESSOR, in the same commit that moves
  the shell — the discipline 04.1 established for every invariant its vendor chunk contradicted. The
  successor asserts what stays both true and enforceable with a single product: the shell sources
  contain no HAOO literal, and every product fact is read from the product definition. The
  withdrawn half — that the collection demonstrates reuse across more than one product — is named as
  withdrawn on the assertion itself rather than deleted, so a reader learns what stopped being
  proven and why.
  — **Reversibility:** one-way — the reuse assertion cannot be restored without a second product;
  restoring it would mean re-deriving Phase 1 D-17 and Phase 2 D-11 against a collection that does
  not exist.

- **D-06:** ZERO-PAPER HUB's Products section is fed by an inline card record in its own registry —
  name, tagline, preview image, and the `haoo.online` URL — with no build-time dependency on the
  HAOO repository. PROD-01 stays true: a visitor still discovers HAOO from the ZERO-PAPER HUB home
  page. Accepted cost, named rather than mitigated: the card copy and HAOO's own copy can drift, and
  nothing checks that they agree.
  — **Reversibility:** reversible — a ~20-line literal.

### Data Controller and Relationship Copy

- **D-07:** HAOO continues to state its parent relationship on its own domain — "A ZERO-PAPER HUB
  product" stays in `src/products/copy.ts:158` and `src/products/haoo.ts:475`. Only the URL changes.
  Rationale recorded for downstream agents: the disclosure must name ZERO-PAPER HUB as the data
  controller regardless of domain (D-08), so dropping the relationship from the visible page would
  mean the visitor first meets the parent inside the privacy text.

- **D-08:** The measurement disclosure and form copy name ZERO-PAPER HUB explicitly as who operates
  HAOO and receives the data, alongside the existing statement that analytics data is processed in
  the United States (04.1 SC6). This is accuracy, not preference: per PROJECT/company profile,
  ZERO-PAPER HUB is a sole proprietorship and HAOO is a product, not a separate legal entity — there
  is no legal person for controllership to move to. A domain change does not change the controller.
  — **Reversibility:** one-way — a disclosure that named a different controller and then shipped
  would be a published privacy statement that cannot be un-published.

- **D-09:** The changed privacy and relationship copy passes a BLOCKING human checkpoint before it
  ships, matching the precedent of Phase 2's collection notice and Phase 3 D-16. The Kenya Data
  Protection Act 2019 sign-off recorded as an open blocker in `02-VALIDATION.md:91` is unresolved,
  and a change of origin for the site that does the collecting falls within what that sign-off must
  cover. An executor may not clear this gate on its own judgment.

### Visitor and Measurement Continuity

- **D-10:** The stranded browser context is ACCEPTED AS A RESET, and the storage key is renamed off
  the `zph.` prefix, which is a misnomer on `haoo.online`. `localStorage` is origin-scoped, so every
  existing `zph.haoo.ctx.v1` record becomes unreachable at the new origin: returning visitors are
  treated as `first` with no interaction flags. Phase 3 D-11 already defines exactly that state as a
  legitimate coarse answer, so this is a known-good degradation, not a new failure mode. Cross-origin
  migration was considered and REJECTED on a recorded cost: an iframe-plus-postMessage handover is
  mechanically indistinguishable from the cross-site identity technique MEAS-03 prohibits and the
  disclosure denies. The rename is handled as a schema-version event under Phase 3 D-10.
  — **Reversibility:** one-way — the records at the old origin are unreachable from the new one the
  moment the domain changes; no later decision can recover them.

- **D-11:** HAOO keeps the SAME PostHog project across the move. **Rationale corrected 2026-09-05
  after measurement.** This decision was originally justified on the claim that no deploy had ever
  run against a capturing build, taken from 04.1-VERIFICATION.md. That claim was already stale when
  it was read: the three repository variables were created at 10:50-10:51Z, a green deploy ran at
  10:57Z against `7ba2c89`, and the published artifact at `www.zero-paperhub.com/products/haoo/`
  now serves the `posthog-sdk` vendor chunk. The project therefore DOES accumulate event history,
  starting 2026-09-05.

  The decision is unchanged and now rests on a stronger footing: there is real history to preserve,
  and reusing the project keeps the owner gates 04.1 closed — the recorded processor approval, the
  client-IP discard setting, and the pinned token — rather than re-opening them against an empty
  successor. **Consequence downstream agents must handle:** the project will contain events from
  BOTH hostnames across the cutover. Reporting must not silently present a hostname-mixed count as
  a single-site figure; whether that is handled by a HogQL filter, a documented cutover date, or an
  explicit note in the report is left to planning.
  — **Reversibility:** costly — the project now holds data, so a later move to a fresh project
  abandons the history rather than migrating it.

- **D-12:** The retired `/products/haoo/` path serves a minimal recovery document from the
  ZERO-PAPER HUB repository: `<link rel="canonical">` to the HAOO page, a meta-refresh, and a
  visible link for any visitor the refresh does not carry. GitHub Pages serves no 301, so this is
  the closest available equivalent. It is a static document, not a route: it carries no measurement,
  no form, and no product content.

### Claude's Discretion

Downstream agents retain discretion over: the mechanics of the clone-and-prune (tooling, order of
deletions, how the two trees are verified disjoint); the new storage key's exact name and version
token; the recovery document's wording and markup; the inline card record's shape in the
ZERO-PAPER HUB registry; how the retired-reuse successor assertion is structured in the suite; and
the order in which URL literals are re-pointed — provided D-01 through D-12 hold and the
success criteria in ROADMAP.md are met.

Three items are factual corrections rather than decisions and need no gate: the form subject line
and source line in `src/products/haoo.ts:583-585` (which names
`www.zero-paperhub.com/products/haoo/`), the `CNAME` file, and the `<link rel="canonical">` and
Open Graph URLs in `products/haoo/index.html`.

</decisions>

- **Withdraw with a named successor.** 04.1 resolved four build-output cases this way rather than

  `none` and captures nothing. This is what makes the reduced ZERO-PAPERHUB build safe by default.

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- The domain is `haoo.online` — already owned, and already referenced by the product's own contact
  surface (`info@haoo.online`, `manage.haoo.online`), so the product's identity is already there.
- The split is deliberately sequenced BEFORE Phase 5. Phase 5's four success criteria are all
  assertions about a deployed hostname; running it before the move would spend its evidence on an
  arrangement about to change. Phase 5 becomes the split's acceptance test.
- **Timing, corrected 2026-09-05.** An earlier draft of this file said production still served
  Phase 1 only and that 14 commits were unpushed. Both were false by the time they were written.
  Measured instead: `origin/main` is at `7ba2c89`, a green Pages deploy ran at 10:57Z, and the live
  HAOO page serves the full Phase 2-04.1 journey including the PostHog vendor chunk. The split is
  therefore migrating a LIVE, CAPTURING funnel, not a brochure page. It is still early — the deploy
  is hours old, so little is indexed and no meaningful engagement history has accumulated — but the
  redirect at the retired path (D-12) and the storage reset (D-10) now affect real visitors rather
  than hypothetical ones, and 04.2 should not be deferred on the assumption that nothing is live.

</specifics>
