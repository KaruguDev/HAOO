## Visual Direction

HAOO leads visually inside the familiar ZERO-PAPER HUB shell. The shared header and footer retain the parent company's restrained green/blue identity and structure; the product body switches decisively to HAOO indigo, navy, pale-blue surfaces, property imagery, and product voice. Display the relationship label `A ZERO-PAPER HUB product` beside the HAOO identity in the hero and `HAOO is a ZERO-PAPER HUB product` in the footer. Every displayed, metadata, and accessible-name occurrence of the parent brand must be exactly `ZERO-PAPER HUB`.

The page should feel calm, credible, and operational rather than playful: broad light surfaces, strong navy headings, restrained shadows, rounded cards, and one clear action hierarchy. Adapt the brochure's visual identity; do not reproduce its fixed print-sheet layout.

### Imagery

- Use the supplied HAOO logo without recoloring, stretching, masking, or placing it on a low-contrast surface.
- Use the supplied property hero photograph as a supporting visual, not as a substitute for HTML copy. Desktop: place it in the right column at a 4:5 aspect ratio with `object-cover`; mobile: place it below the hero actions at 4:3 with a crop that keeps the foreground property professional visible.
- Use the supplied landscape brochure preview for the home-page product card, mobile brochure panel, and social image. Preserve its aspect ratio and use `object-cover` only where the full image is also available through the brochure controls.
- Image alternative text must describe function and subject without repeating adjacent copy. Decorative crops use empty `alt`; the brochure preview uses `HAOO property-management brochure preview`.

**Source:** D-13 through D-17; inspected HAOO source assets; existing ZERO-PAPER HUB shell.

## Spacing Scale

| Token | Value | Usage |
|-------|-------|-------|
| xs | 4px | Icon gaps, inline padding |
| sm | 8px | Compact element spacing |
| md | 16px | Default element spacing |

| xl | 32px | Grid gaps and desktop card padding |
| 2xl | 48px | Mobile major section breaks |
| 3xl | 64px | Desktop major section breaks and page-level spacing |

Exceptions: interactive links and controls have a minimum 44px by 44px hit area; this accessibility target is not a layout-spacing token.

Layout rules:

- Content container: `max-w-7xl`, centered, with 16px horizontal padding below `sm`, 24px from `sm`, and 32px from `lg`.
- Reading copy: maximum 680px line width; hero copy: maximum 620px.
- Section padding: 48px vertical below `md`; 64px vertical from `md` upward.
- Card grids: 16px gap on mobile, 24px on tablet, 32px on desktop.
- Card padding: 24px on mobile and 32px from `md` upward.
- Do not use arbitrary spacing values except for the 44px minimum target. Existing shell dimensions may remain where unchanged.

**Source:** standard 8-point default; 44px target is an accessibility exception.

## Typography

Use exactly these four sizes and two weights in new Phase 1 product UI:

| Role | Size | Weight | Line Height |
|------|------|--------|-------------|
| Body | 16px | 400 | 1.5 |
| Label | 14px | 600 | 1.4 |
| Heading | 28px | 600 | 1.2 |
| Display | 40px | 600 | 1.1 |

- Body includes descriptions, card copy, contact details, and helper text.
- Label includes navigation, eyebrow text, button text, metadata such as `PDF · 2.1 MB`, and compact card headings.
- Heading is used for `h2` and `h3`; maintain semantic order even where a smaller visual role is appropriate.
- Display is reserved for the product-page `h1` and may wrap naturally; never truncate headings or CTA labels.
- Uppercase is limited to short labels and the required brand spelling. Body text remains sentence case.

**Source:** default values, constrained by the existing Inter font and upstream requirement for exact, restrained typography.

## Information Architecture and Responsive Layout

### Home-page Products section

1. Add `Products` to the home navigation and link it to `#products`.
2. Place the Products section after Services and before Values so product discovery occurs before the company-wide conversion close.
3. Render HAOO as one full-width featured card from centralized product data. Desktop uses a balanced 5/7 copy-and-image split; mobile stacks copy before image.
4. Card content order: `Featured product` label, HAOO identity, `Run the business—not the paperwork.`, audience line naming landlords and property managers together, then `Explore HAOO`.
5. The container is a reusable one-column product collection at one item and may become a two-column grid at `lg` when future products exist. Do not hard-code a HAOO-only page structure into the collection.

### HAOO product page

Use this fixed section order:

1. Shared ZERO-PAPER HUB header with `Back to ZERO-PAPER HUB`, followed by HAOO anchors for Benefits, Capabilities, Brochure, and Onboarding.
2. HAOO hero: identity/relationship, outcome-first `h1`, equal mention of landlords and property managers, assisted-primary and self-service-secondary actions, and property image.
3. Pain-to-benefit overview: show the paperwork/fragmentation problem first, then concise business outcomes.
4. Six capability cards: Rent & payments; Properties & units; Leases & screening; Maintenance; Vacancy marketplace; Reports & communication.
5. Four-step ordered rental journey: Fill vacancies with confidence; Move in with clarity; Make every month easier; Grow with visibility.
6. Mid-page onboarding choice block.
7. Brochure section after the complete guided HTML overview.
8. Final onboarding block and subtle product relationship footer.

At widths below `md` (768px), use one-column flow, a compact HAOO anchor menu, stacked full-width actions, and no inline PDF viewer. At `md`, capability cards use two columns. At `lg` (1024px), hero and onboarding choices use two columns, capability cards use three columns, and the desktop PDF object may render. No surface may create horizontal page overflow at 320px or 200% zoom.

**Source:** D-01 through D-17; PROD-01 through PROD-04; existing Tailwind breakpoints.

## Interaction Contract

- Primary assisted path is a native WhatsApp anchor with the generic starter text: `Hello HAOO, I would like help choosing the best way to get started.` Never include page data, analytics data, or personal values in the URL.
- Call uses visible `+254 702 188 044` and `tel:+254702188044`. Email uses visible `info@haoo.online` and `mailto:info@haoo.online`.
- Self-onboarding uses visible destination `manage.haoo.online` and `https://manage.haoo.online/`.
- Render all four onboarding links unconditionally. No analytics, storage, PDF, form, or JavaScript readiness state may hide, disable, delay, or intercept navigation.
- `Open brochure` opens the PDF in a new tab and communicates that behavior in its accessible name; include `rel="noopener"`. `Download brochure` uses the native same-origin `download` attribute and shows `PDF · 2.1 MB` as adjacent expectation text.
- Default, hover, focus-visible, and active states must remain distinguishable without relying on scale. Hover may shift color/shadow; focus uses a 2px accent ring with a 2px light offset.
- Motion is optional enhancement only. Use opacity/translate transitions no longer than 200ms, disable reveal transforms under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, and never delay access to actions or content.
- Navigation landmarks, headings, ordered steps, links, and `<object>` fallback must remain meaningful with CSS, images, PDF embedding, and JavaScript independently unavailable.

**Source:** ONBD-01 through ONBD-05; D-06 through D-12; existing accessibility conventions.

## Accessibility Contract

- Use one `h1`; section headings follow sequentially without skipping for visual size.
- Provide `Skip to HAOO content` as the first focusable control on the product page.
- Keyboard order follows DOM/reading order. Do not make cards clickable through nested links or JavaScript handlers.
- Every icon-only menu control has an accessible name. Content icons are `aria-hidden="true"`.
- Focus indicators remain visible on light, navy, and image-adjacent surfaces.
- Keep touch targets at least 44px square and preserve zoom/reflow to 200% without loss of content or action.
- The HTML overview is the complete accessible product explanation; the PDF is supplemental.
- New-tab behavior is disclosed for `Open brochure`; external contact and self-onboarding links otherwise use normal same-tab navigation unless the user agent takes over the protocol.

**Source:** QUAL-02/QUAL-03 quality direction, existing project accessibility conventions, and brochure progressive-enhancement requirements.
