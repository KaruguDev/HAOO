**Generalization cue detected.** The noun `product` moved from HAOO-specific to product-generic across `01-02`, `01-03`, and `01-04`: `ProductDefinition` was introduced as the contract, `registry.ts` was built to hold many, `ProductsSection` was proven with a second product — while the product-generic *components* kept naming HAOO directly. The now-primary noun is **`ProductDefinition`**, and specifically `ProductDefinition.name` and `ProductDefinition.slug`.

**Decision: `promote`.**

Rationale: the shell is already fully prop-driven for every fact except its own product name, so promoting `ProductDefinition` to the sole source of product identity removes the last eleven literals without introducing a new abstraction — the alternative, `add-alongside`, would mean keeping the HAOO literals as defaults behind a new optional prop, which is precisely the coincidental correctness the verifier flagged.

Accepted debt: none carried. `ProductHeader`'s prop widens from `productName: string` to `product: ProductDefinition` so it can reach `slug` for its menu id; `BrochurePanel` keeps its narrower `brochure` plus `productName` pair because it owns no DOM id and needs no slug. That asymmetry is the one piece of untidiness accepted, and what would force revisiting it is `BrochurePanel` ever needing a second product-derived value.

**Invariant test suggested and adopted:** `ProductPage` renders correctly for every product in the registry, not just HAOO — implemented in Task 3 as a loop over `PRODUCTS` plus a synthetic non-registry product, so the contract holds today with one product and keeps holding when the registry grows.
</assumption_delta_decision>
